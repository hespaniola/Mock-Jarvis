01 — Visuals

Light, motion & real-time 3D

Wireframes, particles and post-processing running at 60 fps in the browser. The drone beside this panel is built from a few hundred edges and drawn with additive light — no textures, no models.

  • Shaders

    GLSL particle systems, bloom and custom blend passes.

  • Procedural geometry

    Shapes assembled from primitives and code, not files.

  • Motion

    Damped springs and easing that make things feel physical.

02 — Code

Systems that hold up

Same particles, new purpose. Under the hood the morph re-samples both models every frame, so the scroll bar is a scrubber — run it backwards and the drone comes home.

  • Tooling

    Build pipelines, automation and developer experience.

  • Web platforms

    Fast, accessible front ends backed by sensible APIs.

  • Experiments

    Small sharp prototypes — like this page.

03 — Robotics

Kinematics you can touch

Move your cursor: the arm solves inverse kinematics every frame — base yaw, then a two-link solve for shoulder and elbow, with the wrist keeping the gripper on target. Press and hold to close the gripper.

  • Inverse kinematics

    Analytic solvers, joint limits and smooth targeting.

  • Control

    Damped tracking that stays stable at any frame rate.

  • Simulation

    Prototype motion on screen before it touches hardware.
